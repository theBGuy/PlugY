- The installer asks which Diablo II folder to add PlugY4Kolbot to, so it can go into
  any copy of Diablo II, and offers to patch `Game.exe` for D2Bot and kolbot:
  `Game.exe` then starts PlugY by itself, with PlugY's files beside it. Each install
  gets its own entry in Apps & features; uninstalling the one that patched `Game.exe`
  restores it, and leaves your other installs as they are.
