- **Quest drops on every boss kill.** On 1.14d, the new `AlwaysQuestDrops` setting in the `[EXTRA]`
  section of `PlugY.ini` makes Andariel, Duriel, Mephisto, Diablo and Baal drop their quest drop on
  every kill, even after you have finished their quest: better odds of unique, set and rare items,
  and no gold. It works in single player and in the TCP/IP games you host, and is off by default
  (`AlwaysQuestDrops=0`).
