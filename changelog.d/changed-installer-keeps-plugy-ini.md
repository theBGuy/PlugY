- **Upgrades keep your settings.** Upgrading with the installer or the updater
  keeps your existing `PlugY.ini`, so your settings carry over, and adds each
  option a new install's `PlugY.ini` has that yours lacks, such as a new
  feature's section, set to the value PlugY was already using: the new options
  are there to find and change, and nothing plays differently.
