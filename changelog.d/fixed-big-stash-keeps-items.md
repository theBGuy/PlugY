- **With the multi-page stash, turning off the big stash keeps every item.** On
  Diablo II 1.14d, with the multi-page stash (`ActiveMultiPageStash=1`), you can
  turn `ActiveBigStash` off at any time and keep every item: an item that
  doesn't fit the smaller stash moves to a free spot on its page, or to a new
  page at the end of the stash, shown as "Moved items" in your game's language.
